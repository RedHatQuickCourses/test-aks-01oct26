Overview of RHEL for SAP Solutions

Red Hat Enterprise Linux (RHEL) for SAP Solutions provides a standardized, automated infrastructure designed to support SAP workloads, including SAP S/4HANA, across on-premises and cloud environments.

Key components of the RHEL for SAP Solutions subscription include:

  • RHEL Foundation: The core enterprise Linux operating system.

  • High Availability: The High Availability Add-on for maintaining continuous SAP service uptime.

  • Management & Insights: Tools for lifecycle management (Smart Management), proactive risk analytics, and automated mitigation (Red Hat Insights).

  • Automation: RHEL System Roles for SAP and integration with Red Hat Ansible Automation Platform to streamline SAP deployments and day-2 operations.

  • SAP Optimization: Pre-configured SAP-specific libraries and configurations to ensure optimal system performance.

  • Extended Lifecycle: Update Services for SAP Solutions, providing support for specific RHEL minor releases for up to four years.

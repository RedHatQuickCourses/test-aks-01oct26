Welcome to this overview of the Red Hat portfolio for SAP.

Red Hat offers an automated, end-to-end open hybrid cloud infrastructure specifically designed to support both SAP and non-SAP workloads. Whether you are working on-premises or in cloud environments, this portfolio enables your organization to standardize your SAP S/4HANA deployments on a single, highly reliable platform.

There are three key components that make up this portfolio.

First is Red Hat Enterprise Linux for SAP Solutions. This is a comprehensive, bundled subscription that includes the base operating system, the High Availability Add-on, Smart Management, and Red Hat Insights. These tools work together to provide proactive risk mitigation for your environment.

Second, we focus on automation and lifecycle management. By utilizing RHEL System Roles for SAP and the Red Hat Ansible Automation Platform, you can automate complex tasks such as migrations, initial deployments, and ongoing day-two operations.

Finally, the portfolio provides SAP-specific optimizations. This includes specialized configurations, necessary libraries, and dedicated update services. These features provide extended support for specific minor RHEL releases, ensuring the long-term stability and performance of your SAP environments.
